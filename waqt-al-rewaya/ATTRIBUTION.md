## Historical facts shown on screen

Only well-documented facts about the case are shown as text. They come from the published Northern
Lighthouse Board accounts of the incident, summarized in standard histories of the case:

- The three keepers on duty: James Ducat, Thomas Marshall, Donald MacArthur (shown as «جيمس دوكات · توماس مارشال · دونالد ماك آرثر»).
- December 1900: the light was reported unlit in mid-December, and the relief vessel *Hesperus* reached the island on **26 December 1900**.
- One set of oilskins was left behind in the quarters. The reconstruction shows one coat on three hooks, under MacArthur's name.

No document, photograph or quotation is invented. The visuals do not claim to be archival.
